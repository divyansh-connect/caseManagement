Client Portal – User Operating Guide

1. Introduction to Your Client Portal

Welcome to your Client Portal! This portal is a simple, secure online dashboard designed for immigration applicants. It allows you to track your petition progress, complete assigned tasks, upload documents, fill out questionnaires, sign agreements, communicate with your legal team, and view post-filing updates without any technical background.

3. Detailed Menu & Button Directory for Clients

1. Case Overview (Case Overview Tab)

  • Purpose: Your main dashboard displaying real-time case status.
  • What you see:
  • Your current Case Stage (out of 6 client-friendly stages).
  • Your Overall Progress Percentage (e.g. 35% Completed).
  • Urgent Action Items banner.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ▶️ Expand Stage Details Button (On stage card) ➔ Expands stage checklist to view completed and pending tasks.

2. Tasks (Tasks Tab)

  • Purpose: Your personal to-do list assigned by your legal team.
  • What you see: List of tasks, due dates, assigned owner (Client or Legal Team), and status (Awaiting Client, Approved/Completed).
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ✅ Mark as Completed Checkbox ➔ Marks task as completed after performing the required action.

3. Documents (Documents Tab)

  • Purpose: Uploading and viewing your academic, personal, and legal files.
  • What you see: Secure digital file vault displaying document names, categories (CV, Degree, Transcript, Publication, Recommendation Letter), upload date, and verification status (Pending Review, Verified, Needs Revision).
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Upload Document Button ➔ Opens Document Upload Modal to:
  1. Select Category (e.g. CV, Degree, Transcript).
  1. Choose PDF or image file from your device.
  1. Click Upload.
  • 📥 Download Document Button (Next to file row) ➔ Downloads uploaded PDF file.

4. Forms & Questionnaires (Forms Tab)

  • Purpose: Completing intake history and signing official documents.
  • What you see: Interactive intake questionnaires and generated USCIS petition form packages.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 📝 Fill Questionnaire Button ➔ Opens USCIS Intake Questionnaire Modal to enter personal, address, and employment history.
  • ✍️ Sign Agreement / Sign Form Button ➔ Opens Electronic Signature Modal with a digital signature pad to sign contracts and forms.

5. Communication & WhatsApp (Communication Tab)

  • Purpose: Contacting your assigned Case Manager and Legal Writer.
  • What you see: Direct message chat box and WhatsApp live chat button.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 💬 Messaging Hub Logs Button (Sidebar sub-menu) ➔ Opens in-app messaging thread.
  • 📱 WhatsApp Live Chat Button (Sidebar sub-menu) ➔ Opens WhatsApp Live Chat Modal for instant messaging.
  • ✉️ Send Message Button ➔ Sends typed message to your legal team.

6. Agreement & Payments (Agreement & Payments Tab)

  • Purpose: Managing your billing plan, invoices, and receipts.
  • What you see: Selected payment plan summary, invoice milestones, due dates, and payment status (Paid, Pending, Overdue).
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 💳 Pay Milestone Button ➔ Initiates payment process for upcoming milestone.
  • 📥 Download Receipt Button (Next to paid invoice) ➔ Downloads official PDF payment receipt.

7. Appointments (Appointments Tab)

  • Purpose: Booking legal strategy calls and video consultations.
  • What you see: Booking calendar and upcoming appointment schedule.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 📅 Book Consultation Button ➔ Opens Appointment Booking Modal to select date, time slot, and generate video meeting link.

8. Post-Filing Updates (Post-Filing Updates Tab)

  • Purpose: Tracking your petition after it is shipped to USCIS.
  • What you see: Express courier tracking number, delivery status, and USCIS Form I-797 receipt details.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 📷 Upload Receipt Notice Button ➔ Uploads scan/photo of official Form I-797 notice received in physical mailbox.
  • 🌐 Check USCIS Online Status Button ➔ Launches official USCIS online case tracking tool using your receipt number.
