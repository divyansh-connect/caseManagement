Administrator (Case Manager) Dashboard – User Operating Guide

1. Introduction to Administrator Control

The Administrator Control Dashboard is the primary operational portal used by Case Managers and Legal Firm Administrators. It allows administrators to onboard clients, set up cases, assign Drafters and QA Reviewers, manage project tasks, monitor payment milestones, schedule strategy appointments, and coordinate final petition filing to USCIS.

2. Navigation Menu & Working of Every Section

3. Detailed Menu & Button Directory for Administrator

1. Dashboard (Dashboard Tab)

  • Purpose: Daily operational control center.
  • What you see: Metric summary cards (Active Cases, Pending Reviews, Overdue Tasks, Recent Updates) and recent case cards.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Create New Case Button ➔ Opens New Case Creation Modal.
  • ➕ Add New Client Button ➔ Opens New Client Form.
  • 📅 Schedule Call Button ➔ Opens Appointment Booking Modal.
  • ✨ Babel AI Petition Drafter (Top Header/Sidebar) ➔ Opens the AI Assistant Modal.

2. Clients (Clients Tab)

  • Purpose: Onboarding and managing client information.
  • What you see: Database of all registered clients, highest degrees, fields, citation counts, and status badges.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Add New Client Button ➔ Opens the onboarding modal to fill:
  • Client Name, Email, Phone, Country of Birth.
  • Current Field (e.g. Artificial Intelligence).
  • Highest Degree (Ph.D., Master's, Bachelor's + 5 yrs).
  • University, Citations, Publications, and Patents counts.
  • 👁️ View Profile Button (On client card) ➔ Opens full client overview.
  • ✏️ Edit Client Button ➔ Modifies client details.

3. Cases (Cases Tab)

  • Purpose: Creating and managing immigration case files.
  • What you see: Filterable table/grid of cases showing Case Number, Client, Category (EB-2 NIW, EB-1A, O-1), Current Stage (1 to 14), Assigned Writer & Reviewer, and Risk Level.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Create New Case Button ➔ Opens New Case Creation Modal to:
  1. Enter Case Number (e.g. NIW-2026-104).
  1. Select Client from dropdown.
  1. Choose Petition Category & Field Category.
  1. Select Target Filing Date & Service Center (Nebraska or Texas).
  1. Assign Petition Drafter & Senior QA Reviewer.
  1. Evaluate Risk Level (Low, Medium, High).
  • 🎓 Resume & Profile Builder Button (Action button on case row) ➔ Opens Resume Building Modal to optimize CV and calculate citation percentiles.
  • 👁️ Open Case Details Button ➔ Opens full Case Detail view showing:
  • Dhanasar 3-Prong Specifications (Prongs 1, 2, and 3).
  • Recommenders List & status.
  • Task List & Document Exhibits.

4. Tasks (Tasks Tab)

  • Purpose: Managing team to-do items and client action items.
  • What you see: Complete list of tasks, assigned roles, priority badges (Urgent, High, Medium, Low), due dates, and completion status checkboxes.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Add Task Button ➔ Opens Task Modal to assign work to Drafter, Reviewer, or Client.
  • ✅ Mark as Completed Checkbox ➔ Toggles task completion.
  • 🏷️ Filter by Assigned Role Dropdown ➔ Filters tasks by Client, Writer, or Reviewer.

5. Documents (Documents Tab)

  • Purpose: Reviewing client uploads and legal evidence.
  • What you see: File vault displaying file names, categories (CV, Degree, Publication, Recommendation Letter, Forms), exhibit numbers, file sizes, and status (Pending Review, Verified, Needs Revision, Approved).
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Upload Document Button ➔ Opens NewDocModal to upload PDFs/images and assign Exhibit Numbers.
  • 📥 Download File Button ➔ Downloads file to local device.
  • 🔄 Change Status Button ➔ Updates document verification status.

6. Reviews & Approvals (Reviews Tab)

  • Purpose: Managing QA legal audit queue.
  • What you see: Submitted legal petition memorandums awaiting reviewer signoff.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 👁️ Inspect Petition Draft Button ➔ Opens legal review modal.
  • ✅ Approve Package Signoff Button ➔ Grants final clearance for physical printing and shipment.

7. Communication & WhatsApp (Communication Tab)

  • Purpose: Messaging clients and legal staff.
  • What you see: Case message threads and live chat logs.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 📱 WhatsApp Live Chat Button (Sidebar sub-menu) ➔ Opens WhatsApp Live Chat Modal.
  • 💬 Messaging Hub Logs Button (Sidebar sub-menu) ➔ Opens full case communication logs.
  • ✉️ Send Message Button ➔ Sends in-app message.

8. Payments (Payments Tab)

  • Purpose: Milestone billing and invoice management.
  • What you see: Paid, pending, and overdue payment milestones per case.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 💳 Record Payment Button ➔ Opens invoice payment modal to record offline/online payment.
  • 📥 Download Receipt Button ➔ Downloads PDF receipt for client.

9. Appointments (Appointments Tab)

  • Purpose: Scheduling legal consultations and strategy calls.
  • What you see: Interactive calendar with booked time slots and consultation notes.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 📅 Book Consultation Button ➔ Opens Appointment Booking Modal to select date, time slot, and meeting link.

10. Templates, Reports & Settings (Templates, Reports, Settings Tabs)

  • Purpose: Template management, case velocity reports, and firm settings.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Create Template Button ➔ Adds new petition letter template.
  • 📊 Export Velocity Report Button ➔ Downloads stage progress analytics.
  • ⚙️ Save Settings Button ➔ Saves preferences.
