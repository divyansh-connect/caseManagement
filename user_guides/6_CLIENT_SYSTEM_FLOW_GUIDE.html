Welcome to your Immigration Case Portal! This portal is your personal, secure online dashboard where you can track your visa petition progress, upload documents, fill out forms, communicate with your legal team, and stay updated every step of the way.

2. Your Petition Process: 6 Simple Steps

Your visa petition process is divided into 6 clear steps. You can see which step you are currently on at any time on your portal dashboard.

Step 1: Onboarding & Document Collection

  • What You Do:
  1. Log into your portal.
  1. Review and sign your Engagement Agreement.
  1. Select your preferred Payment Plan.
  1. Upload your current Curriculum Vitae (CV), academic degrees, transcripts, and employment letters.
  • Result: Our team reviews your profile and prepares your custom evidence checklist.

Step 2: Case Strategy & Recommendation Letters

  • What You Do:
  1. Proposed Endeavor Review: Review the summary statement written by our team describing your professional work in the U.S., and approve it.
  1. Recommender Details: Submit the names and CVs of 3 to 5 expert recommenders (supervisors, professors, industry leaders, or independent experts).
  1. Letter Review & Signatures: Once our team drafts your custom recommendation letters, download them from your portal, send them to your recommenders for signature on official letterhead, and upload the signed copies back to your portal.

Step 3: Questionnaires & USCIS Forms

  • What You Do:
  1. Go to the Forms tab in your portal and complete the simple online questionnaire (personal details, address history, work history).
  1. Our team automatically transfers your information onto official USCIS petition forms.
  1. Download the completed form package, print it, sign in blue ink where required, and upload the signed pages back.

Step 4: Legal Petition Review (Fact-Checking)

  • What You Do:
  1. Our legal team drafts your comprehensive Petition Memorandum (the core legal document submitted to USCIS).
  1. You will receive a copy of the draft in your portal.
  1. Review the draft to verify that all personal dates, job titles, publication numbers, and project details are 100% accurate.

Step 5: Final Filing & Mailing

  • What You Do:
  1. Clear any remaining balance on your payment plan.
  1. Confirm your physical shipping address.
  1. Our team prints, binds, indices all exhibits, and ships your complete physical petition package to USCIS via trackable express courier.

Step 6: Post-Filing & Status Tracking

  • What You Do:
  1. View your express courier tracking number in your portal to confirm physical delivery to USCIS.
  1. When USCIS sends your official Form I-797 (Receipt Notice) in the mail, upload a photo or scan of it to your portal.
  1. Use the receipt number to track your official USCIS status directly through your portal.

3. Portal Navigation Guide (What Each Tab Does)

Tab Name Icon What it is for What you need to do
Overview 📊 Your main dashboard showing your case status, overall progress percentage, and urgent alerts. Check this tab whenever you log in for quick status updates.
My Tasks 📋 Your to-do list showing action items assigned to you by the legal team. Click on open tasks (e.g. "Upload Degree Certificate"), complete the action, and mark as done.
Documents 📁 Your secure digital file cabinet. Click Upload Document, choose file category (e.g. CV, Degree, Letter), and upload your PDFs.
Forms & Questionnaires 📝 Online forms for your personal and work details. Fill out missing fields accurately and review prepared USCIS forms.
Payments 💳 Invoices and payment management. Choose payment plans, view paid receipts, and pay upcoming installments.
Messages / WhatsApp 💬 Direct chat with your dedicated Case Manager and Legal Writer. Send messages, ask questions, or launch direct WhatsApp chat.
Appointments 📅 Scheduling calendar for strategy calls and consultations. Pick an available date/time slot to book a video meeting with your legal team.
Post-Filing 📮 Activated after filing to track courier shipments and receipt notices. View tracking numbers and upload official letters/notices received from USCIS.
Settings ⚙️ Personal account settings. Update your contact email, phone number, or change password.

4. Easy How-To Instructions

How to Upload a Document

  1. Click Documents in the side menu.
  1. Click the blue Upload Document button.
  1. Select the file category (e.g., CV, Degree, Recommendation Letter).
  1. Choose the file from your computer or phone.
  1. Click Upload. Your file is now safely saved in your portal!

How to Complete a Task

  1. Click My Tasks in the side menu.
  1. Look for tasks labeled Awaiting Client.
  1. Click on the task name to read instructions.
  1. Complete the required action (such as uploading a file or filling a form).
  1. Click Mark as Completed.

How to Send a Message to Your Case Team

  1. Click Messages in the side menu.
  1. Type your question in the text box at the bottom.
  1. Click Send (or click the green WhatsApp button for instant messaging).

How to Book a Strategy Call

  1. Click Appointments in the side menu.
  1. Select a date on the calendar.
  1. Choose an available time slot.
  1. Click Confirm Booking. You will receive a video call link automatically.

5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I know what I need to do right now?

A: Look at the Overview dashboard or open My Tasks. Anything requiring your attention will be listed there with clear instructions.

Q: What file format should I use for uploading documents?

A: PDF is the best format for all legal documents, degrees, and signed letterhead letters. Images (PNG/JPG) are also accepted for official notices.

Q: Why do some forms need a signature in blue ink?

A: USCIS requires original physical signatures in blue ink on specific immigration forms to prevent fraud.

Q: What should I do if I receive a letter or notice from USCIS in my physical mailbox?

A: Take a clear photo or scan of all pages and upload it immediately under the Post-Filing or Documents tab. Our legal team will review it right away!
