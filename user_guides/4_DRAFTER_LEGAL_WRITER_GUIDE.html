Drafter (Legal Petition Writer) Workspace – User Operating Guide

1. Introduction to Drafter Workspace

The Drafter Workspace is designed for Legal Petition Writers, Researchers, and Case Authors. It focuses specifically on analyzing client evidence, formulating Proposed Endeavor statements, managing expert recommenders, utilizing AI assistance to write recommendation letters, and authoring legal Petition Memorandums (under Dhanasar 3-Prong or EB-1A frameworks).

2. Navigation Menu & Working of Every Section

3. Detailed Menu & Button Directory for Drafter

1. Dashboard (Dashboard Tab)

  • Purpose: Overview of your assigned drafting workload and immediate deadlines.
  • What you see: Active assigned cases, upcoming task due dates, and recent document uploads by clients.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ✨ Babel AI Petition Drafter (Top Header/Sidebar) ➔ Opens the AI Assistant Modal for generating recommendation letters, summarizing technical papers, and outlining Dhanasar arguments.

2. My Assigned Cases (My Assigned Cases Tab)

  • Purpose: Accessing cases specifically assigned to you by the Administrator.
  • What you see: List of cases showing Case Number, Client Name, Category (EB-2 NIW, EB-1A), Current Stage, and Target Filing Date.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • 👁️ Open Case File Button ➔ Opens full Case Detail view showing:
  • Dhanasar 3-Prong Specifications:
  • Prong 1: Substantial Merit & National Importance (score 1-100, impact areas).
  • Prong 2: Well Positioned (education track, key achievements, citation percentile).
  • Prong 3: On Balance Beneficial to Waive Job Offer & PERM.
  • EB-1A Criteria Checklist: (Prizes, Membership, Media, Judging, Scholarly Articles, High Salary, etc.).
  • Recommenders Tab: Manage expert recommenders.
  • Documents & Exhibits Tab: Indexed client evidence.

3. Recommender Management (Case Detail ➔ Recommenders Tab)

  • Purpose: Managing expert recommendation letter drafting and signatures.
  • What you see: List of recommenders, their organizations, relationship type (Independent Expert, Academic Advisor, Industry Leader), CV status, and letter drafting status.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Add Recommender Button ➔ Opens NewRecommenderModal to enter:
  • Recommender Name, Title, Organization, Relationship.
  • CV Received toggle.
  • Key Contributions Mentioned.
  • ✨ AI Draft Letter Button ➔ Uses AI to generate a custom 3-4 page recommendation letter tailored to the recommender's profile and Dhanasar prongs.
  • 🔄 Change Recommender Status Dropdown ➔ Updates status (Identified ➔ Outreach Sent ➔ Drafting Letter ➔ Letter Signed ➔ Verified).

4. Research & Evidence (Research & Evidence Tab)

  • Purpose: Reviewing client uploaded academic, employment, and publication records.
  • What you see: Uploaded CVs, degree certificates, transcripts, publication lists, citation reports, and patents.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ➕ Upload Research Document Button ➔ Uploads reference papers or exhibit notes.
  • 📥 Download File Button ➔ Downloads client documents to local computer for drafting reference.
  • 🏷️ Assign Exhibit Number Button ➔ Tags document as Exhibit (e.g. Exhibit 1-1, Exhibit 2-4).

5. My Tasks (My Tasks Tab)

  • Purpose: Managing your personal drafting assignments and deadlines.
  • What you see: Drafting tasks assigned to you with due dates and priority tags (Urgent, High, Medium, Low).
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ✅ Mark as Completed Checkbox ➔ Marks drafting task as completed.

6. Petition Drafts (Petition Drafts / Templates Tab)

  • Purpose: Writing, editing, and managing legal petition memorandums.
  • What you see: Library of standardized templates and draft petition documents.
  • Buttons & Their Actions:
  • ✏️ Edit Draft Button ➔ Opens draft editor to write or edit the 25-40 page petition memo.
  • 📤 Submit for QA Review Button ➔ Sends completed petition draft to the Senior QA Reviewer Dashboard and updates workflow stage to Review & Approval.
